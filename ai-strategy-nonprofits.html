AI Strategy and Enablement for Nonprofits

Use AI with purpose. Build capacity. Advance your mission.

Artificial intelligence can help nonprofits research funding opportunities, strengthen fundraising, reduce administrative burdens, and make better use of limited resources. But adopting AI without a clear strategy can lead to inaccurate information, inconsistent results, privacy concerns, and tools that staff members do not use.

Results One helps nonprofits move from experimenting with AI to using it strategically, responsibly, and effectively.

We develop practical AI strategies, workflows, tools, policies, and training tailored to your mission, people, programs, and organizational capacity. Whether your organization is just beginning its AI journey or ready to expand its use, we help you identify the right opportunities and create a realistic path forward.

AI Should Support Your Mission — Not Distract From It

Nonprofit leaders are being asked to accomplish more with fewer resources. Staff members are managing grant deadlines, donor communications, program reporting, administrative responsibilities, and growing community needs.

AI can help — but only when it is connected to a real organizational goal.

Results One helps your organization answer important questions:

  • Where can AI deliver the greatest value?
  • Which processes should be improved or automated first?
  • What information can – and cannot – be entered into an AI tool?
  • How can staff verify AI-generated information?
  • Which tools are appropriate for the organization’s budget and needs?
  • How will leadership measure results?
  • What policies and safeguards should be established?
  • How can AI-generated content and tools remain accessible and inclusive?

We turn these questions into a clear AI strategy your organization can understand and implement.

AI for Grant Research and Proposal Development

Build a More Strategic and Efficient Grant Pipeline

AI can help nonprofit organizations identify, evaluate, and pursue funding opportunities more efficiently. Results One helps your team integrate AI into the grant lifecycle without compromising accuracy, organizational voice, or funder requirements.

Services may include:

  • AI-supported funding-opportunity research
  • Grant prospect identification and screening
  • Grant-fit and go/no-go assessments
  • Eligibility, deadline, award, and requirement verification processes
  • Funder-priority and prospect analysis
  • Grant pipeline development and tracking
  • Proposal outlines and first-draft development
  • Needs statements, program descriptions, and organizational-background narratives
  • Proposal review for clarity, consistency, and alignment
  • Reusable grant-content libraries
  • Customized grant-research and proposal assistants
  • Prompt libraries and standardized grant workflows

Important: AI can support the research and writing process, but it cannot guarantee that information is accurate or that an organization will receive funding. Results One builds independent verification and human review into every grant-related AI workflow.

AI Policies and Responsible-Use Governance

Protect Your Organization, Your People, and Your Reputation

AI can generate inaccurate information, unsupported claims, biased content, and privacy risks. Nonprofits also have a responsibility to protect information related to donors, employees, clients, partners, and the people they serve.

Results One helps organizations establish practical AI policies and safeguards addressing:

  • Approved and prohibited AI uses
  • Confidential and sensitive information
  • Donor, client, employee, and beneficiary privacy; accuracy and source verification
  • Hallucinations and unsupported claims
  • Bias and equitable outcomes
  • Copyright and intellectual property
  • Human review and approval
  • Transparency and disclosure
  • Accessibility of AI-generated content
  • Vendor and tool evaluation
  • Accountability and ongoing oversight

Our goal is to help your organization encourage responsible innovation without creating unnecessary fear or complexity.

Our Approach

A Practical Path From Exploration to Implementation

  1. Discover: We meet with leadership and staff to understand your mission, goals, challenges, current workflows, existing tools, and organizational readiness.
  2. Prioritize: We identify the AI opportunities with the greatest potential value and determine which initiatives are realistic based on your capacity, budget, and risk level.
  3. Design: We develop your AI strategy, policies, prompts, workflows, content systems, or customized assistants.
  4. Enable: We train employees, provide implementation guidance, and create the resources needed to support responsible adoption.
  5. Evaluate: We review results, gather staff feedback, measure progress, and refine the strategy as your organization’s needs evolve.
