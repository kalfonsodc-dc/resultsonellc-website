Foster Accessibility and Inclusivity

Fostering accessibility and inclusiveness is our practice of ensuring inclusion across all levels of an organization. We help organizations prioritize accessibility so that all employees and clients have an equal opportunity to reach their highest potential. We ensure that our training materials and work products are accessible to everyone! We are committed to delivering high-quality training, digital accessibility testing, and consulting designed to build a workplace culture where everyone feels comfortable.

Full service minority and woman owned consultant firm based in the District of Columbia.
