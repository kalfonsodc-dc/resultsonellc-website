ADA Lawsuits Are Rising—Is Your Website at Risk?

← Back to Blog

Many organizations don’t realize their website is a legal liability.

Under ADA Title III, your website is considered part of your business. If it’s not accessible, you can be sued.

And this is not just happening to large corporations.

👉 Small businesses are increasingly being targeted.

Common Website Accessibility Issues

Some of the most common violations include:

  • Missing alt text for images
  • Poor color contrast
  • Buttons not labeled properly
  • Forms that are not accessible
  • Missing headings and structure

Federal Contractors Face Even More Risk

If your organization works with the federal government, you must comply with:

✓️ Section 508
✓️ Provide a VPAT (Voluntary Product Accessibility Template)

Failure to comply can result in:

  • Lost contracts
  • Disqualification from RFPs

Don’t Wait for a Lawsuit

The best time to fix your website is before someone files a complaint.
