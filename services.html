Our Services

Results One LLC helps organizations in the Washington, DC region and across the United States remove barriers and build workplaces where everyone can participate. Our work falls into seven service areas, delivered in person and on remote platforms and tailored to each work environment and workforce.

Accessibility Learning Series

Workshops on ADA and website accessibility, assistive technology, accessible content creation, PDFs, and virtual meetings. Sessions run one to three hours and include videos, interactive working sessions, quizzes, and real-world scenarios.

AI Strategy for Nonprofits

Practical AI strategy, workflows, tools, policies, and training tailored to your mission, people, programs, and capacity — including AI for grant research and proposal development.

Digital Accessibility Testing & Training

Automated and manual testing and remediation guidance across platforms, delivered by certified accessibility professionals, including testers who use assistive technology daily.

Disability & Inclusion

Training that increases awareness of people living with disabilities, dispels common myths, and helps teams engage respectfully and confidently.

Leadership for Organizational Success

Two complementary offerings: INSIGHT™, our leadership intelligence process, and Leadership Development Training for current and emerging leaders.

Professional Development Training

Courses covering accessibility and accommodation, anti-harassment policy, civility, conflict resolution, disability sensitivity, hostile work environments, and more.

Workforce Development Training

Job readiness skills, career pathways and advancement, and workplace accessibility — plus change management, employee resource training, executive coaching, and strategic planning.
