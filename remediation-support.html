Accessibility Remediation Support

Digital Accessibility Services › Accessibility Remediation Support

Finding Accessibility Problems Is Only the Beginning. We Help You Fix Them.

An accessibility audit tells you where barriers exist. Accessibility remediation is how you remove them.

Results One LLC provides hands-on digital accessibility remediation and remediation support for organizations that need to correct accessibility barriers in websites, PDFs, Microsoft Office documents, forms, presentations, and other digital content.

Whether your organization has received an accessibility audit from Results One, has findings from another accessibility vendor, or has identified inaccessible content internally, our team can help you move from a list of problems to accessible, usable content.

Our goal is straightforward:

Identify the barrier. Fix the issue. Retest the content. Confirm the result.

Do You Fix the Accessibility Issues—or Do We?

Either approach is available.

Some organizations have internal web developers, designers, communications professionals, or document teams who can make accessibility corrections themselves. In those cases, Results One can provide technical remediation guidance, answer questions, review proposed fixes, and retest the work after corrections are completed.

Other organizations need Results One to perform the remediation directly. For supported content and document formats, our accessibility specialists can make the necessary corrections and return remediated files to your organization.

For websites and applications, we can work collaboratively with your developers and vendors by providing specific remediation instructions, examples, technical guidance, and validation testing.

This flexibility allows your organization to determine the approach that best matches your internal capabilities, timeline, budget, and technical environment.

Document Accessibility Remediation

Documents are one of the most common—and often overlooked—sources of digital accessibility barriers. Results One provides accessibility remediation for individual documents as well as large document libraries. Our services may include remediation of:

  • PDF documents
  • Microsoft Word documents
  • PowerPoint presentations
  • Forms and fillable PDFs
  • Reports and publications
  • Policies and procedures
  • Applications and public forms
  • Training materials
  • Meeting materials
  • Marketing and communications materials
  • Tables, charts, and graphics
  • Other electronic documents

Depending on the document, remediation may address tag structure, headings, lists, tables, reading order, alternative text, links, document language, form fields, color contrast, bookmarks, metadata, and other accessibility requirements.

We can also evaluate the source document when available and recommend whether remediation should occur in the source file or final PDF.

Website & Application Remediation Support

When a website or application audit identifies accessibility barriers, development teams need more than an error code. Results One works with technical teams to translate accessibility findings into actionable remediation. Our support may address issues involving:

  • Keyboard accessibility
  • Focus order and focus visibility
  • Forms and labels
  • Headings and page structure
  • Images and alternative text
  • Links and buttons
  • Navigation
  • Color contrast
  • Accessible names and descriptions
  • Tables
  • Dynamic content
  • Modal dialogs
  • Error messages
  • Multimedia
  • ARIA implementation
  • Screen reader compatibility

We can work directly with your developers, website vendor, application team, or other technical partners to explain what needs to change, why it matters, and how the corrected experience should function.

What If Another Company Performed the Audit?

You do not have to start over.

Results One can review accessibility findings produced by another qualified vendor, an internal accessibility team, or an existing assessment and determine whether we can use those findings as the basis for remediation.

If additional testing is necessary to validate an issue or understand the appropriate fix, we will identify that during scoping.

This allows organizations that already have accessibility reports to move directly toward remediation rather than automatically purchasing another complete audit.

Large-Scale Document Remediation

Have hundreds—or thousands—of documents? Results One can develop a phased remediation strategy for large document libraries.

Rather than treating every file as equally urgent, we can help organizations identify priorities based on factors such as public use, frequency of access, program importance, legal or regulatory requirements, document age, and whether content remains active.

A large-scale project may include:

  1. Inventory — catalogue what exists and what condition it is in.
  2. Prioritize — rank by public use, legal requirement, and whether the content is still active.
  3. Remediate — correct the documents in priority order.
  4. Quality review — check the corrected files against accessibility requirements.
  5. Deliver — return the remediated files to your organization.
  6. Monitor — keep track of new and updated content over time through ongoing monitoring.

This approach can be especially valuable for government agencies and other organizations preparing for accessibility compliance initiatives that have accumulated extensive libraries of PDFs and other digital documents.

How Long Does Remediation Take?

Turnaround depends on the number, length, format, complexity, and current accessibility condition of the documents or digital content.

A primarily text-based document may require significantly less remediation than a document containing complex tables, forms, charts, graphics, scanned content, or unusual layouts.

Large batches of documents can be scheduled in phases, allowing organizations to prioritize the most important content first.

For websites and applications, timelines depend on the number and severity of findings, development resources, release schedules, and whether Results One or the client’s technical team is responsible for implementing corrections.

After reviewing the materials, Results One establishes the anticipated scope, schedule, responsibilities, and deliverables before remediation begins.

How Is Remediation Priced?

Remediation pricing is based on the scope and complexity of the work required. Document projects may be priced based on factors such as:

  • Number of documents
  • Number of pages
  • Document complexity
  • File type
  • Forms and interactive elements
  • Tables and graphics
  • Scanned or image-based content
  • Alternative text requirements
  • Required turnaround
  • Volume of documents
  • Specialized remediation requirements

Projects may be scoped as an individual document, a batch of documents, an hourly engagement, or a larger phased remediation project.

Website and application remediation support may be priced according to the number and complexity of findings, required technical assistance, testing requirements, and project duration.

Results One provides a defined scope and pricing structure before work begins so clients understand what is included.

Do You Retest After Remediation?

Yes. Validation is an important part of the remediation process. Correcting an accessibility issue does not automatically mean that the barrier has been resolved successfully.

Results One can conduct quality assurance and regression testing after remediation to verify that identified issues have been appropriately addressed and that corrections have not introduced new accessibility barriers.

Depending on the engagement, validation may include manual testing, keyboard testing, document accessibility checks, and assistive technology testing using tools such as JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver, and other appropriate technologies.

For client-implemented website or application fixes, Results One can retest the affected components or pages and document whether the original findings have been resolved.

The project scope will clearly identify whether retesting and validation are included or priced separately.

What Do You Receive?

Depending on the engagement, deliverables may include:

  • Remediated accessible documents
  • Technical remediation guidance
  • Corrected source or final files, as applicable
  • Remediation status tracking
  • Developer consultation
  • Quality assurance review
  • Regression testing
  • Validation of corrected accessibility issues
  • Summary of completed remediation
  • Identification of issues requiring additional work

For large engagements, Results One can also provide progress reporting so project teams and leadership can see what has been completed, what remains outstanding, and what should happen next.
