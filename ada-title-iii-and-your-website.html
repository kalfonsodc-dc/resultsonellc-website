ADA Title III and Your Website: Why Your Business Could Be at Risk

← Back to Blog

Think ADA Only Applies to Physical Spaces? Think Again.

If your business serves the public, your website is legally considered part of your business under ADA Title III.

That means:
👉 If your website isn’t accessible, you could be at risk of lawsuits.

The Rise of Digital Accessibility Lawsuits

Thousands of businesses—large and small—have faced legal action due to inaccessible websites.

Common industries impacted:

  • Retail
  • Hospitality
  • Healthcare
  • Financial services
  • Professional services

And yes—small businesses are not exempt.

What Makes a Website “Non-Compliant”?

Common issues include:

  • Buttons and links not labeled for assistive technology
  • Missing or incorrect headings
  • Poor color contrast
  • Forms without labels
  • No keyboard navigation

Automated tools alone only catch ~25% of issues—manual testing is essential.

What About Federal Contractors?

If your business works with the federal government, you must also comply with:

Section 508 Requirements

  • Provide a VPAT (Voluntary Product Accessibility Template)
  • Demonstrate accessibility of digital products/services

Failure to comply can:

  • Disqualify you from contracts
  • Impact funding opportunities

What You Should Do Right Now

  • Run an accessibility scan
  • Conduct a manual audit
  • Fix high-risk issues immediately
  • Create an accessibility statement
