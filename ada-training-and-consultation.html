ADA Training & Consultation

Go Beyond Compliance. Build Accessibility Into the Way You Work.

Understanding the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) is about more than knowing the law. It is about understanding how accessibility, inclusion, and equal opportunity translate into everyday decisions, services, programs, facilities, communications, and customer experiences.

Results One LLC provides ADA Training and Consultation led by two certified ADA trainers who bring both professional expertise and lived and personal experience with disability.

This combination allows us to move beyond theory and compliance checklists. We help organizations understand what accessibility looks like in real-world situations—and what they can do to create more welcoming, inclusive, and accessible environments.

Our ADA Training Services

Our interactive ADA training can be customized for your organization, leadership team, employees, managers, or customer-facing staff.

Training topics can include:

  • ADA Fundamentals — Understanding the ADA, its purpose, and organizational responsibilities under Title II and Title III
  • Disability Awareness & Inclusion — Understanding different types of disabilities, including visible and non-apparent disabilities
  • Reasonable Accommodations — Understanding the accommodation process and how to respond appropriately
  • Accessible Customer Service — Creating positive and equitable experiences for customers, visitors, and community members with disabilities, which is where Title III obligations are met in practice
  • Effective Communication — Communicating with people who are Deaf, hard of hearing, blind, have low vision, or have other communication-related disabilities
  • Digital Accessibility & the ADA — Understanding accessibility requirements for websites, documents, PDFs, social media, videos, and other digital content, including WCAG 2.1 Level AA and Section 508
  • Accessible Meetings & Events — Planning meetings, conferences, programs, and events that are inclusive from the beginning
  • Disability Etiquette & Respectful Interaction — Building staff confidence and reducing uncertainty when interacting with people with disabilities
  • ADA for Managers & Supervisors — Helping leaders understand their responsibilities and create inclusive workplaces
  • ADA for State & Local Government — Helping public entities understand their Title II obligations and incorporate accessibility throughout programs, services, and communications

ADA Consultation

Sometimes organizations need more than training.

Results One provides customized ADA consultation to help organizations identify accessibility gaps, strengthen policies and practices, and determine practical next steps.

Consultation may include:

  • ADA accessibility assessments
  • Policy and procedure review
  • Reasonable accommodation process review
  • Accessibility strategy development
  • Accessible program and service delivery
  • Meeting and event accessibility planning
  • Digital accessibility guidance
  • Staff and leadership consultation
  • Accessibility implementation planning
  • Recommendations for improving disability inclusion across the organization
