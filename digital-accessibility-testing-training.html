Digital Accessibility Services

Results One LLC helps organizations create digital experiences that are accessible, inclusive, and usable by people with disabilities. Our services support government agencies, nonprofits, corporations, educational institutions, and other organizations seeking to strengthen accessibility, address compliance obligations, and make accessibility part of everyday operations.

Why it matters:

  1. Legal compliance — Laws such as the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act, and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) require digital content to be accessible. Non-compliance can result in lawsuits, penalties, and reputational damage.
  2. Inclusion and equity — Over 1 billion people globally live with a disability. Accessibility testing ensures digital spaces are inclusive, removing barriers that prevent full participation in education, employment, healthcare, and daily life.
  3. Improved user experience — Accessible design benefits all users, not just those with disabilities. Features like captions, keyboard navigation, and clear layout improve usability for everyone, including older adults and mobile users.
  4. Brand reputation and reach — Accessible digital platforms demonstrate a commitment to diversity and inclusion, attracting a broader audience and enhancing brand loyalty.
  5. Future-proofing — As technology evolves and legal standards become stricter, organizations that invest in accessibility now are better prepared for future regulations and innovations.

Our Digital Accessibility Services

Accessibility Audits & Assessments

Automated, manual and assistive technology testing of websites, mobile apps and digital documents against WCAG, Section 508 and applicable ADA requirements, with a prioritized remediation report.

Accessibility Remediation Support

We fix the barriers, or guide your team to fix them: PDF and Office document remediation, website and application support, large-library projects, and retesting.

Accessibility Training & Workshops

Customized sessions for executives, developers, content creators, communications and procurement teams, delivered as workshops, Lunch & Learns, virtual training or leadership briefings.

Ongoing Compliance Monitoring

Monthly, quarterly or risk-based monitoring: automated scans, targeted manual testing, regression testing and periodic reporting so new barriers are caught early.

VPAT/ACR Development

Evaluation of digital products and preparation of an Accessibility Conformance Report that gives procurement professionals meaningful information, not marketing copy.

Policy & Strategy Consulting

Policies, governance, roadmaps, procurement requirements and accessibility statements that turn individual projects into an organization-wide accessibility program.

Community & User Accessibility Testing

Task-based testing by IAAP-certified and DHS Trusted Tester professionals, including testers who are blind or visually impaired and use assistive technology daily.

AI & Accessibility

Training and consultation on where AI removes barriers for people with disabilities — and on accuracy, bias, privacy and the risk of assuming automation is accessible.

Video & Multimedia Accessibility

Audio description, closed captioning, caption and description remediation, accessible transcripts, and phased remediation plans for large video libraries.

Who Does the Testing

All of our testers are certified by the International Association of Accessibility Professionals and Department of Homeland Security Trusted Testers with over 10 years of experience. Our certified testers are also visually impaired or blind, considered a gold standard in accessibility testing.

We hold our own site to the same standard we test against. Our accessibility statement sets out where we conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA, the limitations we know about, and how to tell us if something does not work for you.
