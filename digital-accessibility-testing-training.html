Digital Accessibility Testing & Training

Digital accessibility testing and training are critical for ensuring that all users—including people with disabilities—can access and interact with digital content, tools, and platforms. Digital Accessibility Testing and Training ensures that websites, applications (mobile apps, software), videos, and digital content (social media, documents, presentations) are usable by people with disabilities and compliant with legal standards such as the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act.

Here’s why it matters:

  1. Legal Compliance: Laws such as the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act, and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) require digital content to be accessible. Non-compliance can result in lawsuits, penalties, and reputational damage.
  2. Inclusion and Equity: Over 1 billion people globally live with a disability. Accessibility testing ensures digital spaces are inclusive, removing barriers that prevent full participation in education, employment, healthcare, and daily life.
  3. Improved User Experience: Accessible design benefits all users, not just those with disabilities. Features like captions, keyboard navigation, and clear layout improve usability for everyone, including older adults and mobile users.
  4. Brand Reputation and Reach: Accessible digital platforms demonstrate a commitment to diversity and inclusion, attracting a broader audience and enhancing brand loyalty.
  5. Future-Proofing: As technology evolves and legal standards become stricter, organizations that invest in accessibility now are better prepared for future regulations and innovations.

In short, digital accessibility testing and training are essential for creating a digital world that is usable, equitable, and compliant.

Results One LLC helps organizations build inclusive, compliant, and user-friendly digital experiences through a full suite of accessibility services:

1. Accessibility Audits & Assessments

  • Manual and automated testing of websites, mobile apps, and digital documents
  • Compliance evaluation against WCAG, ADA, and Section 508 standards
  • Detailed reports with prioritized recommendations for remediation

2. Remediation Support

  • Guidance and hands-on support to fix identified accessibility issues
  • Collaboration with developers, designers, and content teams
  • Accessible document remediation (PDFs, Word, PowerPoint, etc.)

3. Accessibility Training & Workshops

  • Custom sessions for web teams, content creators, executives, and staff
  • Topics include: Creating Accessible Content, Website Accessibility 101, Social Media & Accessibility, and more
  • Lunch & Learns, virtual sessions, and certification-based training available

4. Ongoing Compliance Monitoring

  • Monthly or quarterly scans and updates
  • Retesting and reporting to track improvements and ensure sustained compliance

5. VPAT/ACR Development

  • Creation of Voluntary Product Accessibility Templates (VPATs) and Accessibility Conformance Reports (ACRs) for digital products and services

6. Policy and Strategy Consulting

  • Development of digital accessibility roadmaps, internal policies, and procurement checklists
  • Strategic guidance for integrating accessibility into digital transformation initiatives

7. Community and User Testing

  • Real-world testing with users who rely on assistive technologies such as screen readers, switch devices, or magnifiers

8. AI & Accessibility

  • Provide training on the Intersection of AI and Accessibility: Learn how AI tools (e.g., voice assistants, image recognition, automated captions) support independent living and access to information for people with disabilities.
  • Apply Practical Use Cases: See real-world examples of accessible AI tools that support communication, mobility, and workplace inclusion.

All of our testers are certified by the International Association of Accessibility Professionals and Department of Homeland Trusted Testers with over 10 years of experience. Our certified testers are also visually impaired or blind, considered a gold standard in accessibility testing.
