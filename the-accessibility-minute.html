The Accessibility Minute

One Minute. One Accessibility Insight. One Step Forward.

Digital accessibility shouldn't be overwhelming.

The Accessibility Minute gives government professionals practical, easy-to-understand guidance to help ensure that everyone, including people with disabilities, can access and interact with digital content, tools, and services.

Sign up today and spend one minute each week making digital accessibility more achievable.

Accessibility Made Practical

You don't need to be an accessibility expert to make a difference.

Whether you work in IT, communications, procurement, web development, or agency leadership, you're likely responsible for digital content and services that residents depend on.

The challenge is knowing where to start.

The Accessibility Minute breaks accessibility into small, manageable steps so you can make meaningful progress without becoming a full-time compliance expert.

Each issue gives you something practical you can understand and act on.

What You'll Get in Each Issue

One minute. Three useful takeaways. A practical step toward better digital access.

  • Accessibility Insight: Understand what accessibility requirements mean for your organization and the people you serve.
  • Practical Action: Get a simple action you can take to improve the accessibility of your website, documents, forms, social media, or digital services.
  • Helpful Resources: Discover a useful checklist, tool, reference, or resource you can return to when you need it.

Why Digital Accessibility Matters

Imagine needing to complete an online application, find important information, register for a service, or access a government document, only to discover that the digital experience doesn't work with your assistive technology.

For many people with disabilities, this isn't hypothetical. It's an everyday reality.

Accessible digital services help people access information and opportunities independently, with dignity, and without unnecessary barriers.

Accessibility isn't simply about checking a compliance box.

It's about making sure your digital services work for the people who rely on them.

Why I Created The Accessibility Minute

For more than 20 years, I've helped organizations identify and remove barriers that prevent people with disabilities from accessing information, services, and opportunities.

But digital accessibility is more than my profession. It's personal.

As the mother of a daughter who is blind, I've experienced firsthand what happens when websites, online forms, PDFs, and digital services aren't accessible.

I've seen how something that seems simple to one person can become a frustrating obstacle for someone else.

That's why I created The Accessibility Minute.

My goal is to make accessibility easier to understand, easier to prioritize, and easier to put into practice.

Because creating accessible digital experiences shouldn't have to feel impossible.
